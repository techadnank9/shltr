Step 1 · the survivor

A phone photo of the room.

JPG, HEIC or a slow 10-second video. The file is only ever opened inside a sealed, throwaway sandbox.

Step 2 · photo sandbox on VM 2

The photo becomes a room.
In metres.

Depth Anything V2 runs in a microVM with its own kernel and no network. Out comes a textured mesh: 250,000 vertices from one photo.
